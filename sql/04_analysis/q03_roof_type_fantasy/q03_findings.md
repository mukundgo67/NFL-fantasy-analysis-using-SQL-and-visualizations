\#\# Retractable roofs and scoring

Retractable-roof games ranked among the two highest-scoring roof types for  
\[three\] of the four position groups. This probably says more about closed roofs  
than about retractables: in this data, 110 of 126 retractable-roof games (about  
87%) were played with the roof closed, so these averages mostly describe indoor
